Privacy Policy

Rock Paper Scissors for Watch

This privacy policy explains how Rock Paper Scissors for Watch handles data.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information, location data, contacts, photos, or browsing data.

Local Data Storage

The app stores gameplay statistics and user preferences locally on your Apple Watch and/or paired device. This data stays on your device unless you manually clear it.

In-App Purchase

If the app includes an in-app purchase in the future, purchase entitlement checks may use Apple-provided transaction data to unlock features. We do not store payment card details.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@example.com.
