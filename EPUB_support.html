常见问题

转换出来还是乱码怎么办？

App 会自动检测编码，但少数文件的特征不明显。请回到转换页面，在「文本编码」里手动换成 GBK、GB18030、Big5 或 UTF-16 之后再点「重新解析」，看到正文正常了再生成。如果换了一圈还是乱码，把文件发给我们，我们帮你确认。

章节没有被识别出来？

点「查看分章预览」看看实际识别结果。目前支持「第 X 章 / 回 / 节」「卷 / 部 / 篇」「序章 / 楔子 / 番外 / 尾声」「Chapter N」等常见写法。如果你的小说用了别的格式，可以在「章节识别」里切换规则；实在识别不到时，整本书会作为一章导出，不会丢内容。

生成的 EPUB 里没有目录，或者目录点不动？

生成的是标准 EPUB 3，同时带 nav 和 NCX 两套目录，Apple Books、多看、掌阅等主流阅读器都能识别。如果某个阅读器里看不到目录入口，通常是那个阅读器本身没有做目录按钮，可以试试换一个阅读器验证。

转换好的 EPUB 怎么放进 Apple Books 或 Kindle？

在转换完成页点「分享 / 存储到文件」：想放进 Apple Books，直接选「图书」；想留一份，选「存储到文件」。Kindle 的话，把导出的 EPUB 用「Send to Kindle」推送即可。

支持哪些格式和编码？

格式支持 TXT 与 EPUB 双向互转。文本编码支持 UTF-8、UTF-16、GBK、GB18030、GB2312、Big5、Shift-JIS、EUC-KR 和 Windows-1252，基本覆盖了中文小说的常见来源。

转换后的文件存在哪里？

保存在 App 自己的 Exports 目录里，转换完成页和首页的「最近导出」都能直接分享或储存。想清理的话，到「设置 → 存储 → 清空导出文件」。注意清空只影响导出目录，不会动到书架里的书。

为什么转换一本大书要等几秒？

分章、排版和生成目录需要把全文读一遍，6MB 左右的 GBK 小说大约 1 秒。书籍越大、章节越多，时间会相应变长，转换过程中请不要退出 App。

阅读进度会在设备之间同步吗？

不会，目前所有数据都只保存在这台设备上，不上传也不跨设备同步。换手机时，书籍需要重新导入。

买了永久会员但没有解锁？

请到「设置」的会员卡片点击「恢复购买」，并确认当前的 Apple ID 与购买时使用的是同一个。如果是刚买完仍然没解锁，把 App 完全退出再打开一次通常就好了。

免费试用是怎么算的？

首次下载后有 24 小时可以免费使用全部功能，到期后需要开通永久会员才能继续转换。试用起点记录在本机，删除重装不会重新开始；永久会员是一次性购买，不会自动续费。

删除 App 会丢数据吗？

会。删除 App 会清除书架、阅读进度、阅读设置和导出文件，且无法恢复，请谨慎操作。已经购买的永久会员不受影响，重装后点「恢复购买」即可找回。

可以只转换其中几章吗？

目前是整本转换。如果只需要一部分，可以先生成完整的 EPUB，再在阅读器里按章跳转阅读，或者把不需要的章节删掉。

FAQ

The converted file still shows garbled text

The app detects the encoding automatically, but a few files are ambiguous. Go back to the conversion screen, set Text Encoding manually to GBK, GB18030, Big5 or UTF-16, then tap Re-analyze and check the preview before generating. If none of them look right, send us the file and we will take a look.

Chapters were not detected

Tap Preview Chapters to see what was found. The app recognises common styles such as 第 X 章 / 回 / 节, 卷 / 部 / 篇, 序章 / 楔子 / 番外 / 尾声, and Chapter N. If your novel uses something else, switch the rule under Chapter Detection. If nothing matches, the whole book is exported as a single chapter, so no content is lost.

The EPUB has no table of contents, or it will not open

The app produces a standard EPUB 3 with both nav and NCX contents, which Apple Books, Google Play Books and most other readers can read. If one particular reader shows no contents entry, it is usually because that reader has no contents button of its own. Try another reader to confirm.

How do I get the EPUB into Apple Books or Kindle?

On the result screen tap Share / Save to Files. Choose Books to send it straight to Apple Books, or Save to Files to keep a copy. For Kindle, push the exported EPUB with Send to Kindle.

Which formats and encodings are supported?

TXT and EPUB convert in both directions. Text encodings include UTF-8, UTF-16, GBK, GB18030, GB2312, Big5, Shift-JIS, EUC-KR and Windows-1252, which covers the usual sources of Chinese novels.

Where do converted files go?

They are kept in the app Exports folder, and both the result screen and Recent Exports on the home screen let you share or save them. To clean up, use Settings, Storage, Clear Exported Files. Clearing only touches the export folder, never your library.

Why does a big book take a few seconds?

Detecting chapters, laying out the text and building the contents means reading the whole file once. A 6 MB GBK novel takes about a second. Larger books and more chapters take longer, so please keep the app open while it works.

Does my reading position sync between devices?

No. Everything stays on this device and is never uploaded or synced. When you move to a new phone you will need to import your books again.

I bought the lifetime membership but it did not unlock

Tap Restore Purchase on the membership card in Settings, and make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. If you just purchased and it still has not unlocked, quitting the app completely and reopening it usually resolves it.

How does the free trial work?

Your first 24 hours include every feature for free. After that, a lifetime membership is required to keep converting. The trial start time is stored on this device, so deleting and reinstalling does not restart it. The membership is a one-time purchase and never renews.

Will I lose data if I delete the app?

Yes. Deleting the app removes your library, reading progress, reading settings and exported files, and it cannot be undone. Please be careful. A purchased membership is unaffected, and Restore Purchase will bring it back after reinstalling.

Can I convert only some chapters?

Conversion is for the whole book. If you only need part of it, generate the full EPUB and jump between chapters in your reader, or delete the chapters you do not want.
