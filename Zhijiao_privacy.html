中文

我们对隐私的态度

一掷 · 筊杯是一款以闽南掷筊习俗为灵感的静心问事 App。我们尽量少收集数据：你的问题、结果和记录都只保存在本机，不需要账号，也不会上传到我们的服务器。

English

Our Privacy Commitment

Crescent Cast is a quiet jiaobei ritual app inspired by the Minnan moon-block tradition. We keep data collection to a minimum: your questions, results, and records stay on your device. There is no account, and nothing is uploaded to our servers.

中文

我们会保存哪些数据

  • 你在签纸上写下的来意或问题。
  • 本次请示的方式：单掷或三圣筊。
  • 每一次掷杯的结果与顺序，以及最终落定结果。
  • 记录的创建时间。
  • 你之后补充的「后来呢」状态、备注，以及答谢备忘的内容、是否已答谢和答谢日期。

以上内容只用于在 App 内展示你的签册与回访记录。

English

What We Store

  • The question or intention you write on the paper.
  • The casting mode: Single Cast or Three Sacred Casts.
  • Each cast result and sequence, and the final settled result.
  • When the record was created.
  • Any follow-up status or notes you add later, plus the contents of your vow/thanks memo, whether you marked it as thanked, and the date.

This information is used only to show your record book and follow-up notes inside the app.

中文

数据保存在哪里

你的签文与记录保存在 App 的本机沙盒中，不会发送给我们或任何第三方。当前版本没有账号系统、云同步或服务器数据库。

如果你使用 iCloud 备份或电脑备份，iOS 可能会按照你的备份设置把 App 数据包含在设备备份中。该备份由 Apple 与你的设备管理，我们无法访问。

English

Where Your Data Lives

Your records are stored in the app's local sandbox on your device. They are not sent to us or to any third party. This version has no account system, cloud sync, or server database.

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, iOS may include app data in your device backup according to your settings. That backup is managed by Apple and your device; we cannot access it.

中文

我们不会收集什么

  • 我们不会收集你的姓名、手机号、通讯录、定位、麦克风、相机或照片。
  • App 内没有广告，没有第三方分析、追踪或广告 SDK。
  • 我们不会把你的签文、结果或备注出售、分享给任何第三方。
English

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, microphone, camera, or photos.
  • There are no ads and no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We never sell or share your questions, results, or notes with anyone.
中文

声音、时间与权限

  • App 会播放一段很短的筊杯落地声，使用系统音频播放；不会录音，也不会请求麦克风权限。声音跟随系统静音键。
  • 祈祷页的早、中、晚场景只读取设备本地时间（05:00、11:00、21:00 切换），不会请求定位权限。
  • 当前版本不会请求相机、相册、通讯录、定位、麦克风、健康或通知权限。
English

Audio, Time, and Permissions

  • The app plays a short landing sound using system audio playback. It does not record audio or request microphone access. The sound follows the silent switch.
  • The prayer scene reads only your device's local time to choose morning, day, or evening (changing at 05:00, 11:00, and 21:00). It does not request location access.
  • This version does not request camera, photo library, contacts, location, microphone, health, or notification permissions.
中文

数据保留与删除

你可以在「签册」里左滑单条记录并点「删除」，或打开签文详情后点「删除这签」。删除 App 会清除本机保存的全部签文、结果和备注，且无法恢复，请谨慎操作。

当前版本没有内置的导出或云备份功能；如果需要保留记录，请使用系统的整机备份，并自行确认备份设置。

English

Data Retention and Deletion

In Records, swipe left on a record and tap Delete, or open the record and tap Delete Record. Deleting the app removes all locally stored records, results, and notes permanently. Please be careful.

This version does not include in-app export or cloud backup. If you want to keep your records, use a full device backup and review your backup settings.
