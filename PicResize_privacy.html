Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated: 2026-05-21

English

PicResize is designed to help you resize photos and convert image formats directly on your device. We aim to keep data collection to a minimum.

  • The app processes selected images locally on your device.
  • The app does not require an account or sign-in.
  • The app does not intentionally collect personal data.
  • The app does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • The app may request access to your photo library so you can select images and save the processed result.
  • Output images are saved to your photo library only when you choose to do so.

If you contact us by email for support, we may use your message and attachments only to respond to your request.
