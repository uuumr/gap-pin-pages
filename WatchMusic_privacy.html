中文

我们对隐私的态度

手表音乐播放器是一款本地音乐播放 App，支持在 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 之间手动同步本地音乐，并可在任一设备上独立播放。我们尽量只在你的设备之间处理必要的数据，不会为了广告或账号体系而额外收集个人信息。

English

Our Privacy Commitment

Watch Music Player is a local music playback app that lets you manually sync music between your iPhone and Apple Watch and play it on either device. We aim to process only the data needed to deliver playback, and we do not build advertising or account systems that require unnecessary personal data.

中文

我们会收集和保存哪些数据

  • 你手动导入到 App 的本地音乐文件（如 mp3 / m4a / aac / wav / aiff / flac）。
  • 歌曲标题、歌手、时长等基础元数据，以及播放模式等偏好设置。
  • 手机与手表之间手动同步时所需的歌曲标识与音频数据（会转码为 AAC 128kbps 以便在手表上播放）。
  • 永久会员购买状态，用于判断是否解锁手机 3 首、手表 2 首的非会员数量限制。

以上数据主要保存在你的设备本地，并通过 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 之间的系统通信进行同步。

English

What Data We Collect and Store

  • Local music files that you manually import into the app (for example, mp3 / m4a / aac / wav / aiff / flac).
  • Basic metadata such as song titles, artists, and durations, plus preferences like your playback mode.
  • Song identifiers and audio data needed for manual syncing between iPhone and Apple Watch (audio is transcoded to AAC 128 kbps for watch playback).
  • Lifetime membership status, which is used to determine whether the free limits of 3 songs on iPhone and 2 songs on Apple Watch are unlocked.

Most of this data is stored locally on your devices and is synchronized through Apple's system communication between iPhone and Apple Watch.

我们不会收集什么

  • 我们不会主动收集你的姓名、手机号、通讯录、定位、照片、麦克风或相机数据。
  • 我们不会在应用中加入广告追踪，也不会把你的音乐文件或使用数据出售给第三方。
English

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in to a profile.
  • We do not intentionally collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera data.
  • We do not use ad tracking in the app or sell your music files or usage data to third parties.
中文

第三方服务与系统能力

  • Apple StoreKit：用于处理永久会员的一次性非消耗型 App 内购买，商品 ID 为 com.zsz.WatchMusic.PermanentMember。
  • Apple Watch Connectivity：用于在手机和手表之间同步歌曲列表与音频数据。
  • 系统文件选择器：用于让你手动选择并导入本地音乐文件。

我们不会直接获取你的银行卡号、支付密码等敏感支付信息；购买流程由 Apple 负责。

English

Third-Party Services and System Features

  • Apple StoreKit: used for the one-time non-consumable lifetime membership in-app purchase with product ID com.zsz.WatchMusic.PermanentMember.
  • Apple Watch Connectivity: used to sync the song list and audio data between iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • System file picker: used when you manually select local music files to import.

We do not directly access your card number, payment password, or other sensitive payment details. Apple handles the purchase flow.

中文

数据保留与删除

你的音乐文件、播放记录和设置主要保存在本地设备上。手机与手表的音乐库相互独立，你可以分别在两端删除歌曲。删除 App、移除歌曲或重置设备都会清除相关本地数据。

English

Data Retention and Deletion

Your music files, playback history, and settings are primarily stored on your local devices. The iPhone and Apple Watch libraries are independent, so you can delete songs on either device separately. Deleting the app, removing songs, or resetting the device will clear the related local data.
