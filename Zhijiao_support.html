Quick Start

  1. On the prayer screen, press and hold the ring until it is full, then release.
  2. Write your question on the paper. One question at a time; write as much or as little as you like.
  3. Choose Single Cast or Three Sacred Casts.
  4. Wait for the blocks to land, then tap the screen to view the sealed result.
  5. The result is saved to Records automatically. Add follow-up notes and a vow note later.

常见问题

怎么开始一次掷筊？

在祈祷页长按圆环，蓄满后松手，写下来意，再选择单掷或三圣筊。

单掷和三圣筊有什么区别？

单掷以一次结果为准；三圣筊是同一个问题连续三次圣筊。笑筊不计入、可以再掷，阴筊即止。

圣筊、笑筊、阴筊分别是什么意思？

圣筊是一平一凸，表示允许；笑筊是双平，表示笑而不答，可以把问题说得更清楚；阴筊是双凸，表示否定，先停下。

掷出笑筊或阴筊后怎么办？

笑筊可以整理问题后再掷；阴筊不要硬掷，可以先停下，改日或换一种问法。

记录保存在哪里？

保存在本机的签册里，结果落定后自动保存，不会上传到服务器。

怎么删除一条记录？

在签册列表左滑记录并点删除，或打开签文详情后点「删除这签」。

需要账号或网络吗？

不需要账号，核心问事与签册功能都可以离线使用。

怎么切换语言？

App 跟随系统语言，支持英文、简体中文、繁体中文。可以在 iOS「设置 → 通用 → 语言与地区」里切换。

为什么背景会变化？

祈祷页会按本地时间切换早、中、晚场景：05:00、11:00、21:00。

为什么听不到落地声？

请检查媒体音量和静音键。App 使用系统音频播放，会跟随静音键，也不会录音。

删除 App 会丢记录吗？

会。记录只存在本机，删除 App 会清除全部签文与备注，且无法恢复。

可以导出或备份记录吗？

当前版本没有内置导出功能。整机备份可能包含 App 数据，取决于你的系统备份设置；我们无法访问该备份。

结果是预测吗？

不是。它是一个文化仪式与个人记录工具，不构成医疗、法律、投资或赌博建议，也没有下注或奖品。

为什么结果会重复？

App 的结果由仪式逻辑生成，不是科学预测。重点在于停下来把问题问清楚，并把答案记录下来。

怎么反馈问题或建议功能？

发邮件到下方邮箱，附上设备型号、iOS 版本、App 版本、复现步骤和截图，会更容易定位。

FAQ

How do I start a cast?

On the prayer screen, press and hold the ring until it is full, release, write your question, then choose Single Cast or Three Sacred Casts.

What is the difference between Single Cast and Three Sacred Casts?

Single Cast uses one result. Three Sacred Casts asks for three sacred results for the same question. Laughing blocks do not count and may be cast again; a yin block ends the session.

What do Sacred, Laughing, and Yin mean?

Sacred is one flat and one convex block: permitted. Laughing is two flat blocks: the deity smiles but does not answer, so clarify your question. Yin is two convex blocks: no; stop for now.

What should I do after a Laughing or Yin result?

After Laughing, clarify your question and cast again. After Yin, do not force another cast; stop, return another day, or ask in a different way.

Where are my records stored?

In the local Records book on your device. Results are saved automatically and are not uploaded to a server.

How do I delete a record?

In Records, swipe left on a record and tap Delete, or open the record and tap Delete Record.

Does the app need an account or internet?

No account is needed, and the core ritual and Records work offline.

How do I change the language?

The app follows your device language and supports English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. Change it in iOS Settings, General, Language & Region.

Why did the background change?

The prayer scene follows local time: morning, day, and evening, changing at 05:00, 11:00, and 21:00.

Why can't I hear the landing sound?

Check the media volume and the silent switch. The app uses system audio playback, follows the silent switch, and does not record audio.

Will deleting the app delete my records?

Yes. Records are stored only on this device. Deleting the app removes all records and notes permanently.

Can I export or back up my records?

This version has no built-in export. A full device backup may include app data depending on your backup settings; we cannot access that backup.

Is the result a prediction?

No. It is a cultural ritual and personal record tool, not medical, legal, financial, or gambling advice. There are no bets or prizes.

Why do results repeat?

The app generates a ritual outcome, not a scientific prediction. The value is in pausing to ask clearly and keeping a record.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email us with your device model, iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce, and a screenshot if possible.
