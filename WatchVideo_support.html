常见问题

支持哪些视频格式？

支持 mp4 / m4v / mov。上传到手表前会自动转码成适合手表播放的 H.264 + AAC MP4 格式。

如何把视频传到手表？

在 iPhone 上导入视频后，进入“同步”标签选择要同步的视频并点“开始同步”。请保持手机与手表靠近，并先打开过一次手表 App。

传输一直停在“等待手表确认”？

请保持手机与手表连接并让手表 App 停留在前台。若长时间无进展，请重新打开两端 App 后重试。首次同步的视频较大时需要多等一会儿。

视频在手表上不能播放？

请确认视频已显示在手表列表中，并检查手表存储空间是否充足。过大的原始视频会自动转码后再传输。

非会员数量限制是多少？

非会员在手机上最多 3 个、手表上最多 1 个视频。开通永久会员后手机与手表均不限数量。

会员购买后没有生效？

可以点击设置中的“恢复购买”，并确认 Apple ID 登录状态与购买时一致。

FAQ

What video formats are supported?

mp4 / m4v / mov are supported. Videos are automatically transcoded into watch-friendly H.264 + AAC MP4 before transfer.

How do I put videos on my watch?

Import videos on your iPhone, open the Sync tab, choose the videos, and tap Start Sync. Keep the devices close and open the watch app at least once first.

Why is the transfer stuck at “waiting for the watch”?

Keep the devices connected and the watch app in the foreground. If nothing advances, reopen both apps and try again. Large videos can take a while on the first sync.

Why won't a video play on the watch?

Make sure the video appears in the watch list and that the watch has enough free storage. Large originals are transcoded before transfer.

What are the free-tier limits?

Free users can keep up to 3 videos on iPhone and 1 on Apple Watch. Lifetime membership removes the limits on both devices.

Why didn't membership unlock after purchase?

Try Restore Purchases in Settings, and make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.
