常见问题

支持哪些视频和音乐格式？

视频支持 mp4 / m4v / mov；音乐支持 mp3 / m4a / aac / wav / aiff / flac。同步到手表前会按所选画质 / 音质压缩，视频会转码为适合手表播放的 H.264 + AAC MP4，音乐转为 AAC。

支持哪些漫画格式？

压缩包支持 .zip 和 .cbz（.cbz 就是改过扩展名的 zip），不支持 rar / cbr / 7z。压缩包内的图片支持 jpg / jpeg / png / webp / heic / gif / bmp / tiff，按文件名排序作为页序，建议用 001.jpg、002.jpg 这样补零的命名；__MACOSX、.DS_Store 等非图片文件会被自动忽略。

如何把视频和音乐传到手表？

在 iPhone 上导入视频或音乐后，进入“同步”标签，分别勾选要同步的内容并点“开始同步”，两者会一起传到手表。请保持手机与手表靠近，并先打开过一次手表 App。

传输一直停在“等待手表确认”？

请保持手机与手表连接并让手表 App 停留在前台。若长时间无进展，请重新打开两端 App 后重试。首次同步时视频需要先压缩，文件较大时需要多等一会儿。

媒体在手表上不能播放？

请确认内容已显示在手表对应的“视频”或“音乐”列表中，并检查手表存储空间是否充足。如果刚同步完仍无法播放，可以删除该条目后重新同步一次；过大的原始文件会在传输前自动压缩。

手表上视频无法播放怎么办？

如果点开视频没反应、黑屏，或者一直转圈，通常是手表 App 的播放进程卡住了。请在手表上双击数码表冠打开多任务，把「腕上多媒体」向左滑关闭，再重新打开 App，视频一般就能正常播放。

如果重新打开后仍然不行，可以在手表上删除这个视频，再从手机同步一次。

怎样让同步到手表的内容更小？

在设置或同步页选择更低的画质 / 音质（视频可选 360p / 480p / 540p / 720p 或不压缩，音乐可选 128k / 192k / 256k 或原文件）。压缩只会让文件变小：如果压缩后反而更大，App 会自动改用原文件。

音乐播放时返回列表会中断吗？

不会。返回列表后音乐继续播放，手表主页底部会显示当前播放的音乐，点击即可回到播放页，也可以直接暂停或继续。

手表上音乐没有声音？

watchOS 不允许 App 用自带的扬声器播放音乐和视频声音，必须连接蓝牙耳机。先在手表“设置 → 蓝牙”配对耳机，或直接戴上已配对过的耳机再开始播放，声音会自动切换到耳机。

支持哪些小说格式？

支持 txt / md / epub。epub 会在导入时转成带章节的 txt（原文件不改动）；纯文本编码自动识别 UTF-8 和 GB18030，中文小说常见的乱码问题基本不会遇到。单个文件超过 10 MB 会在导入前提示。

在手表上怎么读小说？

点开书籍即可阅读：轻点屏幕下方翻到下一页，轻点上方三分之一翻回上一页，也可以转动数码表冠翻页。点左上角呼出控制层，里面有目录、阅读设置和进度；阅读设置里可以调字号、翻页方式（分页 / 滚动）、自动翻页、表冠反馈、主题颜色和行距。主题里的“纯黑”在 OLED 屏幕上最省电。

小说显示乱码或章节对不上？

先确认文件本身是 txt / md / epub。乱码通常是文件使用了少见的编码（如 Big5），可以转成 UTF-8 后重新导入。章节对不上多见于没有“第 X 章”标题的书，此时全篇会当作一章并按 2 万字分段。

设置里看不到会员价格？

价格来自 App Store，首次打开会短暂显示“正在获取价格…”。如果显示“暂时无法获取会员商品信息”，点下方的“重新获取商品信息”即可重试；仍失败多为网络或商店区域问题，稍后再试或检查网络。

非会员数量限制是多少？

非会员在手机上最多 5 个视频、5 首音乐、5 本漫画和 5 本小说，手表上各 1 个。开通永久会员后手机与手表均不限数量。

会员购买后没有生效？

可以点击设置中的“恢复购买”，并确认 Apple ID 登录状态与购买时一致。

FAQ

What video and music formats are supported?

Video: mp4 / m4v / mov. Music: mp3 / m4a / aac / wav / aiff / flac. Before syncing, media is compressed to the quality you selected — video becomes a watch-friendly H.264 + AAC MP4, and music is encoded to AAC.

What comic formats are supported?

Archives can be .zip or .cbz (.cbz is just a zip with a different extension); rar, cbr and 7z are not supported. Images inside can be jpg / jpeg / png / webp / heic / gif / bmp / tiff. Pages are ordered by file name, so names like 001.jpg, 002.jpg sort correctly; non-image entries such as __MACOSX and .DS_Store are ignored.

How do I put videos and music on my watch?

Import your media on iPhone, open the Sync tab, select the videos and songs you want, then tap Start Sync — both are transferred together. Keep the devices close and open the watch app at least once first.

Why is the transfer stuck at “waiting for the watch”?

Keep the devices connected and the watch app in the foreground. If nothing advances, reopen both apps and try again. On the first sync videos are compressed first, so large files can take a while.

Why won't media play on the watch?

Make sure the item appears in the watch's Videos or Music list and that the watch has enough free storage. If it still won't play right after syncing, delete it and sync once more; large files are compressed before transfer.

What if a video won't play on the watch?

If tapping a video does nothing, the screen stays black or a spinner never finishes, the watch app's playback process is usually stuck. Double-press the Digital Crown to open the app switcher, swipe "Wrist Media" to the left to close it, then open the app again — playback normally works after that.

If it still fails, delete the video on the watch and sync it once more from the iPhone.

How can I make synced media smaller?

Pick a lower quality in Settings or on the Sync screen — video offers 360p / 480p / 540p / 720p or no compression, and music offers 128k / 192k / 256k or the original file. Compression only ever shrinks a file: if the result would be larger, the app sends the original instead.

Does music stop when I go back to the list?

No. Music keeps playing when you return to the list, and the watch home screen shows what's playing at the bottom — tap it to reopen the player, or pause and resume right there.

No sound when playing music on the watch?

watchOS does not let apps play music or video audio through the built-in speaker — Bluetooth headphones are required. Pair them in Settings → Bluetooth on the watch, or put on headphones that are already paired and start playback; audio switches to them automatically.

Which book formats are supported?

txt / md / epub. An epub is converted at import into a chaptered txt (the original file is untouched). Text encoding is detected automatically, so both UTF-8 and GB18030 (common for Chinese novels) decode correctly. Files over 10 MB are flagged before importing.

How do I read a book on the watch?

Tap a book to open it: tap the lower part of the screen for the next page, the top third for the previous one, or turn the Digital Crown. The top-left corner opens the controls, with contents, reading settings and progress. Reading settings cover type size, page mode (paged / scroll), auto page turn, crown haptics, theme and line spacing — the Pure black theme is the most power-efficient on an OLED watch.

Garbled text, or chapters that do not line up?

First check the file really is txt / md / epub. Garbled text usually means an unusual encoding (Big5, for example) — convert it to UTF-8 and import again. Missing chapter breaks are normal for books without “Chapter X” headings: the whole text becomes one chapter, split every 20,000 characters.

Why can't I see a membership price?

The price comes from the App Store, so it may briefly show “Fetching price…”. If it says the product cannot be fetched right now, tap “Retry product info” underneath; repeated failures are usually a network or storefront issue, so check your connection and try again later.

What are the free-tier limits?

Free users can keep up to 5 videos, 5 songs, 5 comics and 5 books on iPhone, and 1 of each on Apple Watch. Lifetime membership removes the limits on both devices.

Why didn't membership unlock after purchase?

Try Restore Purchases in Settings, and make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.
