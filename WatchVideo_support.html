常见问题

支持哪些视频和音乐格式？

视频支持 mp4 / m4v / mov；音乐支持 mp3 / m4a / aac / wav / aiff / flac。同步到手表前会按所选画质 / 音质压缩，视频会转码为适合手表播放的 H.264 + AAC MP4，音乐转为 AAC。

如何把视频和音乐传到手表？

在 iPhone 上导入视频或音乐后，进入“同步”标签，分别勾选要同步的内容并点“开始同步”，两者会一起传到手表。请保持手机与手表靠近，并先打开过一次手表 App。

传输一直停在“等待手表确认”？

请保持手机与手表连接并让手表 App 停留在前台。若长时间无进展，请重新打开两端 App 后重试。首次同步时视频需要先压缩，文件较大时需要多等一会儿。

媒体在手表上不能播放？

请确认内容已显示在手表对应的“视频”或“音乐”列表中，并检查手表存储空间是否充足。如果刚同步完仍无法播放，可以删除该条目后重新同步一次；过大的原始文件会在传输前自动压缩。

怎样让同步到手表的内容更小？

在设置或同步页选择更低的画质 / 音质（视频可选 360p / 480p / 540p / 720p 或不压缩，音乐可选 128k / 192k / 256k 或原文件）。压缩只会让文件变小：如果压缩后反而更大，App 会自动改用原文件。

音乐播放时返回列表会中断吗？

不会。返回列表后音乐继续播放，手表主页底部会显示当前播放的音乐，点击即可回到播放页，也可以直接暂停或继续。

非会员数量限制是多少？

非会员在手机上最多 3 个视频和 3 首音乐、手表上最多 1 个视频和 1 首音乐。开通永久会员后手机与手表均不限数量。

会员购买后没有生效？

可以点击设置中的“恢复购买”，并确认 Apple ID 登录状态与购买时一致。

FAQ

What video and music formats are supported?

Video: mp4 / m4v / mov. Music: mp3 / m4a / aac / wav / aiff / flac. Before syncing, media is compressed to the quality you selected — video becomes a watch-friendly H.264 + AAC MP4, and music is encoded to AAC.

How do I put videos and music on my watch?

Import your media on iPhone, open the Sync tab, select the videos and songs you want, then tap Start Sync — both are transferred together. Keep the devices close and open the watch app at least once first.

Why is the transfer stuck at “waiting for the watch”?

Keep the devices connected and the watch app in the foreground. If nothing advances, reopen both apps and try again. On the first sync videos are compressed first, so large files can take a while.

Why won't media play on the watch?

Make sure the item appears in the watch's Videos or Music list and that the watch has enough free storage. If it still won't play right after syncing, delete it and sync once more; large files are compressed before transfer.

How can I make synced media smaller?

Pick a lower quality in Settings or on the Sync screen — video offers 360p / 480p / 540p / 720p or no compression, and music offers 128k / 192k / 256k or the original file. Compression only ever shrinks a file: if the result would be larger, the app sends the original instead.

Does music stop when I go back to the list?

No. Music keeps playing when you return to the list, and the watch home screen shows what's playing at the bottom — tap it to reopen the player, or pause and resume right there.

What are the free-tier limits?

Free users can keep up to 3 videos and 3 songs on iPhone, and 1 of each on Apple Watch. Lifetime membership removes the limits on both devices.

Why didn't membership unlock after purchase?

Try Restore Purchases in Settings, and make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.
