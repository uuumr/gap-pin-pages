我们如何处理隐私

本游戏是一款完全离线的单机游戏，全部在你的 Apple Watch 本地运行。不需要注册账号，不联网，没有广告，也不收集任何个人信息。

English

Our Approach to Privacy

This is a fully offline, single-player game that runs entirely on your Apple Watch. There is no account to create, no network connection, no advertising, and no collection of personal information.

日本語

プライバシーへの考え方

本ゲームは完全オフラインの一人用ゲームで、すべて Apple Watch 上で動作します。アカウント登録は不要で、通信も広告もなく、個人情報を一切収集しません。

中文

我们不收集什么

  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话号码等个人身份信息
  • 不收集设备标识符、位置、通讯录等信息
  • 不含任何第三方广告、统计或分析 SDK
  • App 不发起网络请求，游戏数据不会离开你的手表
English

What We Don't Collect

  • No personal details such as your name, email, or phone number
  • No device identifiers, location, or contacts
  • No third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs
  • The app makes no network requests, so your game data never leaves your watch

保存在你设备上的内容

只有一项：最高分，用于在结算页显示你的历史纪录。它只保存在 Apple Watch 本地，不会上传到任何服务器。

English

What's Stored on Your Device

Just one thing: your best score, shown on the game over screen so you can chase your record. It is stored locally on your Apple Watch and is never uploaded anywhere.

日本語

English

Deleting Your Data

Deleting the app removes all of the above. Starting a new round does not erase your best score; to clear it, delete and reinstall the app.
