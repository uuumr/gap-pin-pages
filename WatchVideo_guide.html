从导入到在手表上播放，按下面几步走就够了 / From import to playback on the watch, just follow these steps.

中文

快速上手

  1. 先在 iPhone 上导入内容 视频可以从“文件”或相册导入；音乐从“文件”导入；漫画支持 zip / cbz 压缩包（包内图片为 jpg / png / webp / heic 等常见格式）；小说支持 txt / md / epub（epub 会自动转成 txt）。点右上角的“+”即可选择文件。
  2. 选一下画质和外观 在“设置 → 压缩”里选择视频画质、音乐音质和漫画画质。默认档位适合大多数情况，觉得占空间太多就调低一档。 想固定浅色或深色，可以在“设置 → 外观”里选“白色”或“黑色”，默认是白色，不会跟随系统变黑。
  3. 同步到手表 打开“同步”标签，勾选要传的内容（也可以打开“全部视频 / 全部音乐 / 全部漫画 / 全部小说”），点右上角“开始同步”。传输过程中可以随时点“取消”中断。
  4. 在手表上播放 手表 App 里有视频、音乐、漫画、小说四个列表，点任意一项即可开始播放或阅读。首次同步前请先打开过一次手表 App。
English

Quick start

  1. Import on iPhone first Videos can come from Files or Photos, music from Files, comics from zip / cbz archives (pages as jpg / png / webp / heic and similar), and books from txt / md / epub (epub is converted to txt automatically). Tap the “+” button in the top-right corner to pick files.
  2. Pick a quality and a look Choose video, music and comic quality under Settings → Compression. The defaults work well; drop one step down if storage is tight. Under Settings → Appearance you can pin the app to White or Black — it defaults to White and no longer follows the system into dark mode.
  3. Sync to the watch Open the Sync tab, select what you want (or turn on All Videos / All Music / All Comics / All Books) and tap Start Sync in the top-right corner. You can stop a transfer at any time with Cancel.
  4. Play it on the watch The watch app has four lists — Videos, Music, Comics and Books. Tap any item to start playing or reading. Open the watch app at least once before your first sync.

在 iPhone 上

导入

  • 视频：右上角“+”可以选择“从文件导入”或“从相册导入”。支持 mp4 / m4v / mov。
  • 音乐：右上角“+”，可一次选择多个文件。支持 mp3 / m4a / aac / wav / aiff / flac。
  • 漫画：右上角“+”，选择 zip 或 cbz 压缩包。暂不支持 rar / 7z / cbr。
  • 压缩包里放的图片支持 jpg / jpeg / png / webp / heic / gif / bmp / tiff（.tif 同样可以）；按文件名自然排序作为页序，所以 2.jpg 会排在 10.jpg 前面。建议用 001.jpg、002.jpg 这种补零命名，翻页顺序最稳妥。
  • 压缩包里的非图片内容会被自动忽略，比如 macOS 打包时附带的 __MACOSX 目录、.DS_Store 和以 ._ 开头的隐藏文件——不会变成空白页。
  • 小说：右上角“+”，选择 txt / md / epub。epub 会自动转成带章节的 txt，原文件不会改动；编码支持 UTF-8 与 GB18030（中文 txt 常见），会自动识别。
  • 单个文件超过 10 MB 的小说会在导入前提示，漫画压缩包同理 —— 手表解压和翻页都会很吃力，建议换小一点的版本。

管理内容（编辑 / 长按排序）

  • 编辑：点左上角齿轮右边的「编辑」按钮进入编辑状态，点条目即可选中，底部出现「传输到手表 / 重命名 / 删除」和「取消」；继续点其它条目可以多选。重命名和删除都在这里操作。
  • 长按拖动排序：按住任意一条约半秒，这一条会浮起来变成一张卡片跟着手指走，滑过其它条目时就换位，松手即完成；视频、音乐、漫画、小说都支持，全程不会进入编辑状态。
  • 漫画是封面网格，没有左滑菜单，请用长按进入编辑模式后再操作。
  • 「重命名」只在选中一条时可用，多选时显示为灰色。
  • 退出编辑模式：点底部的「取消」、点列表空白处，或者直接切换页签。
  • 删除只影响手机上的文件；已经同步到手表的那一份还在，需要在手表的对应列表里单独删除。

传输中的状态

同步页会显示当前进度和正在处理的内容。需要中断时点右上角的“取消”，正在传的会立刻停下，已经保存到手表的内容会保留。

English

On iPhone

Importing

  • Video: the “+” button offers Import from Files and Import from Photos. Supports mp4 / m4v / mov.
  • Music: tap “+” and pick one or more files. Supports mp3 / m4a / aac / wav / aiff / flac.
  • Comics: tap “+” and choose a zip or cbz archive. rar / 7z / cbr are not supported.
  • Images inside the archive can be jpg / jpeg / png / webp / heic / gif / bmp / tiff (.tif works too). Pages are ordered by file name with numeric awareness, so 2.jpg comes before 10.jpg; zero-padded names like 001.jpg, 002.jpg are the safest bet.
  • Non-image entries are ignored — the __MACOSX folder macOS likes to add, .DS_Store, and hidden files starting with ._ never turn into blank pages.
  • Books: tap “+” and pick a txt / md / epub file. epub is converted to chapters automatically and the original file is left untouched. Both UTF-8 and GB18030 (common for Chinese txt) are detected and decoded.
  • A book over 10 MB is flagged before import, and so is a comic archive of that size — the watch has to unzip and page through it, so a smaller edition reads much better.

Managing items (edit / long-press reorder)

  • Editing: tap the Edit button next to the gear in the top-left to enter edit mode, then tap items to select them. A bar appears at the bottom with Send to Apple Watch, Rename, Delete and Cancel; tap more items to select several. Renaming and deleting both happen here.
  • Reordering: hold an item for about half a second — it lifts into a floating card that follows your finger — and it swaps places as it passes other items; release to drop it. Works for videos, music, comics and books, and never enters edit mode.
  • Comics are a cover grid with no swipe menu; long press a cover instead.
  • Rename is only available with a single item selected and is greyed out for a multi-selection.
  • Leave edit mode with Cancel at the bottom, by tapping empty space, or by switching tabs.
  • Deleting affects the iPhone copy only; anything already synced stays on the watch and has to be deleted from the watch's own list.

While transferring

The Sync tab shows progress and what is being processed right now. Tap Cancel in the top-right corner to stop — the transfer stops immediately and anything already saved on the watch stays there.

在手表上

  • 视频与音乐：列表里点一下开始播放，播放页可以暂停、拖动进度、切换上一个 / 下一个。
  • 音乐后台播放：回到列表不会中断，主页底部会显示正在播放的音乐。
  • 漫画：点开一本可以左右翻页，双指缩放页面；读完的进度会自动记住。
  • 小说：点开即可阅读。轻点屏幕右侧翻到下一页，轻点上方三分之一翻回上一页；也可以转动数码表冠翻页。点左上角会呼出控制层（目录、阅读设置、进度）。
  • 阅读设置（控制层 → 阅读设置）：字号、翻页方式（分页 / 滚动）、自动翻页与间隔、表冠反馈、主题颜色（纸张 / 暖色 / 夜间 / 纯黑）、行距。滚动模式下数码表冠平滑滚动当前章节。
  • 纯黑主题：背景是真正的 #000000，OLED 屏幕上这些像素不发光，长时间阅读最省电；正文用 92% 灰白，比纯白更不刺眼。
  • 阅读进度按「第几章 + 章内位置」记录，改字号或切换分页 / 滚动后仍会停在同一段文字上。
  • 删除：手表列表里点每项右侧的删除按钮，只会从手表移除，手机上的文件不受影响。
English

On Apple Watch

  • Videos and music: tap an item to play. The player lets you pause, scrub, and jump to the previous or next item.
  • Background music: going back to the list keeps the music playing, and the watch home screen shows the current track at the bottom.
  • Comics: tap a comic to read it, swipe to turn pages, pinch to zoom. Your reading position is remembered.
  • Books: open one to read. Tap the lower part of the screen for the next page, the top third for the previous page, or turn the Digital Crown. Tap the top-left corner to bring up the controls (contents, reading settings, progress).
  • Reading settings (controls → Reading settings): type size, page mode (paged / scroll), auto page turn and its interval, crown haptics, theme (paper / sepia / night / pure black) and line spacing. In scroll mode the crown scrolls the current chapter smoothly.
  • Pure black draws a true #000000 background — those pixels stay off on an OLED watch, which is the most power-efficient way to read — with 92% grey-white text that is easier on the eyes than pure white.
  • Progress is stored as chapter + position inside the chapter, so changing the type size or switching between paged and scroll mode keeps you on the same sentence.
  • Deleting: tap the delete button on the right of an item in the watch list. This only removes it from the watch — the file on your iPhone is untouched.

常见问题

同步一直停在“正在查询手表上…”？

说明手机还没收到手表的回包。请确认手表 App 已经打开、手机与手表距离够近，然后点右上角“取消”再重新开始同步。

传完了但手表上没有？

先看同步页是否显示“已完成”。如果显示完成但手表列表为空，请在手表的对应列表下拉或重开一次手表 App；仍未出现就删除该条目重新同步一次。

为什么同步前要压缩这么久？

视频需要先转码成手表能播放的 H.264 + AAC 格式，文件越大耗时越久。压缩只在第一次或更换画质后进行，已经压缩过的会复用结果。

压缩会不会把画质弄得更差？

画质由你选择的档位决定，档位越高越接近原片。App 只会压缩不会放大：如果压缩后反而更大，会自动改用原文件。

手表空间不够怎么办？

在手表上删除不需要的内容，或把画质调低一档后重新同步。几十 MB 以上的视频建议选 360p / 480p。

非会员有什么限制？

非会员手机上最多 5 个视频、5 首音乐、5 本漫画和 5 本小说，手表上各 1 个。开通永久会员后两端都不限数量。

导入或传输超过上限时不会静默失败，会弹出“已达非会员上限”，点“前往会员”直接跳到设置里的会员区块。

EPUB 导入后为什么变成了纯文本？

手表端只读纯文本：epub 在导入时会按 spine 顺序抽出一条条章节文字，存成带章节标记的 txt，原 epub 文件不会改动。这样两端排版一致，也不用在手表上解析 XML。

小说章节是怎么分的？

优先识别“第一章 / 第 1 章 / ## 标题”这类章节行；识别不到标题就当作一整章。单章超过 2 万字会按段落均分成几段，标题显示成“第一章 (1/3)”，避免手表一次排版几千页。

为什么有的页面看起来没排满？

分页和绘制用的是同一套文字引擎，页面边界就是实际显示范围，所以不会出现半行或串行。页面偏短通常是段落刚好结束在那里，下一段从新的一页开始。

FAQ

Sync stays on “Checking what the watch already has…”

The iPhone is waiting for a reply from the watch. Make sure the watch app is open and the devices are close together, then tap Cancel in the top-right corner and start the sync again.

It finished transferring, but nothing shows on the watch?

First check that the Sync tab says it completed. If it did but the watch list is empty, reopen the watch app; if the item is still missing, delete it and sync once more.

Why does compression take so long before syncing?

Videos must be converted to H.264 + AAC so the watch can play them, and larger files take longer. It only happens the first time or after changing quality — already prepared files are reused.

Does compression reduce the quality?

Quality is whatever you selected; higher tiers stay closer to the original. The app only ever shrinks — if the result would be larger, it sends the original file instead.

What if the watch runs out of space?

Delete what you no longer need on the watch, or pick a lower quality and sync again. For videos of tens of MB, 360p or 480p is a good choice.

What are the free-tier limits?

Free users can keep up to 5 videos, 5 songs, 5 comics and 5 books on iPhone, and 1 of each on Apple Watch. Lifetime membership removes the limits on both devices.

Going past a limit is never silent: a “Free plan limit reached” alert explains it, and its “Go to membership” button opens the membership section in Settings.

Why did my EPUB turn into plain text?

The watch reads plain text only, so an epub is flattened at import time: chapters come out in spine order as one txt with chapter markers, and the original epub is untouched. That keeps layout identical on both devices and avoids XML parsing on the watch.

How are chapters split?

Lines like “Chapter 1”, “第 1 章” or “## Title” start a chapter; with no such headings the whole book is one chapter. A chapter longer than 20,000 characters is split evenly at paragraph breaks and titled “Chapter 1 (1/3)”, so the watch never has to lay out thousands of pages at once.

Why does a page look like it isn't full?

Pagination and drawing share the same text engine, so a page boundary is exactly what is on screen — never a half line or a repeated one. A short page usually means the paragraph ended there and the next one starts fresh.
