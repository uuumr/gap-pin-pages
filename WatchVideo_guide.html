中文

快速上手

  1. 先在 iPhone 上导入内容 视频可以从“文件”或相册导入；音乐从“文件”导入；漫画支持 zip / cbz 压缩包。点右上角的“+”即可选择文件。
  2. 选一下画质 在“设置 → 压缩”里选择视频画质、音乐音质和漫画画质。默认档位适合大多数情况，觉得占空间太多就调低一档。
  3. 同步到手表 打开“同步”标签，勾选要传的内容（也可以打开“全部视频 / 全部音乐 / 全部漫画”），点右上角“开始同步”。传输过程中可以随时点“取消”中断。
  4. 在手表上播放 手表 App 里有视频、音乐、漫画三个列表，点任意一项即可开始播放或阅读。首次同步前请先打开过一次手表 App。
English

Quick start

  1. Import on iPhone first Videos can come from Files or Photos, music from Files, and comics from zip / cbz archives. Tap the “+” button in the top-right corner to pick files.
  2. Pick a quality Choose video, music and comic quality under Settings → Compression. The defaults work well; drop one step down if storage is tight.
  3. Sync to the watch Open the Sync tab, select what you want (or turn on All Videos / All Music / All Comics) and tap Start Sync in the top-right corner. You can stop a transfer at any time with Cancel.
  4. Play it on the watch The watch app has three lists — Videos, Music and Comics. Tap any item to start playing or reading. Open the watch app at least once before your first sync.

在 iPhone 上

导入

  • 视频：右上角“+”可以选择“从文件导入”或“从相册导入”。支持 mp4 / m4v / mov。
  • 音乐：右上角“+”，可一次选择多个文件。支持 mp3 / m4a / aac / wav / aiff / flac。
  • 漫画：右上角“+”，选择 zip 或 cbz 压缩包。暂不支持 rar / 7z / cbr。

管理内容

长按任意一条可以进入选中状态，再点其它条目可以多选，底部会出现“传输到手表 / 重命名 / 删除”三个按钮。点屏幕空白处、或者直接切换标签页都会退出选中状态。

传输中的状态

同步页会显示当前进度和正在处理的内容。需要中断时点右上角的“取消”，正在传的会立刻停下，已经保存到手表的内容会保留。

English

On iPhone

Importing

  • Video: the “+” button offers Import from Files and Import from Photos. Supports mp4 / m4v / mov.
  • Music: tap “+” and pick one or more files. Supports mp3 / m4a / aac / wav / aiff / flac.
  • Comics: tap “+” and choose a zip or cbz archive. rar / 7z / cbr are not supported.

Managing items

Long press any row to enter selection mode; tap more rows to select several. A bar appears at the bottom with Send to Apple Watch, Rename and Delete. Tap empty space or switch tabs to leave selection mode.

While transferring

The Sync tab shows progress and what is being processed right now. Tap Cancel in the top-right corner to stop — the transfer stops immediately and anything already saved on the watch stays there.

English

On Apple Watch

  • Videos and music: tap an item to play. The player lets you pause, scrub, and jump to the previous or next item.
  • Background music: going back to the list keeps the music playing, and the watch home screen shows the current track at the bottom.
  • Comics: tap a comic to read it, swipe to turn pages, pinch to zoom. Your reading position is remembered.
  • Deleting: tap the delete button on the right of an item in the watch list. This only removes it from the watch — the file on your iPhone is untouched.

常见问题

同步一直停在“正在查询手表上…”？

说明手机还没收到手表的回包。请确认手表 App 已经打开、手机与手表距离够近，然后点右上角“取消”再重新开始同步。

传完了但手表上没有？

先看同步页是否显示“已完成”。如果显示完成但手表列表为空，请在手表的对应列表下拉或重开一次手表 App；仍未出现就删除该条目重新同步一次。

为什么同步前要压缩这么久？

视频需要先转码成手表能播放的 H.264 + AAC 格式，文件越大耗时越久。压缩只在第一次或更换画质后进行，已经压缩过的会复用结果。

压缩会不会把画质弄得更差？

画质由你选择的档位决定，档位越高越接近原片。App 只会压缩不会放大：如果压缩后反而更大，会自动改用原文件。

手表空间不够怎么办？

在手表上删除不需要的内容，或把画质调低一档后重新同步。几十 MB 以上的视频建议选 360p / 480p。

非会员有什么限制？

非会员手机上最多 3 个视频、3 首音乐、3 本漫画，手表上各 1 个。开通永久会员后两端都不限数量。

FAQ

Sync stays on “Checking what the watch already has…”

The iPhone is waiting for a reply from the watch. Make sure the watch app is open and the devices are close together, then tap Cancel in the top-right corner and start the sync again.

It finished transferring, but nothing shows on the watch?

First check that the Sync tab says it completed. If it did but the watch list is empty, reopen the watch app; if the item is still missing, delete it and sync once more.

Why does compression take so long before syncing?

Videos must be converted to H.264 + AAC so the watch can play them, and larger files take longer. It only happens the first time or after changing quality — already prepared files are reused.

Does compression reduce the quality?

Quality is whatever you selected; higher tiers stay closer to the original. The app only ever shrinks — if the result would be larger, it sends the original file instead.

What if the watch runs out of space?

Delete what you no longer need on the watch, or pick a lower quality and sync again. For videos of tens of MB, 360p or 480p is a good choice.

What are the free-tier limits?

Free users can keep up to 3 videos, 3 songs and 3 comics on iPhone, and 1 of each on Apple Watch. Lifetime membership removes the limits on both devices.
