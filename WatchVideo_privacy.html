中文

我们对隐私的态度

手表视频播放器是一款本地离线视频播放 App，支持从 iPhone 导入视频并同步到 Apple Watch，离线播放。视频文件默认只保存在你的设备本地，不会上传到任何服务器。我们尽量只在你的设备之间处理必要的数据，不会为了广告或账号体系而额外收集个人信息。

English

Our Privacy Commitment

Watch Video Player is a local offline video app that lets you import videos on your iPhone and sync them to your Apple Watch for offline playback. Video files stay on your device by default and are never uploaded to any server. We aim to process only the data needed to deliver playback, and we do not build advertising or account systems that require unnecessary personal data.

中文

我们会收集和保存哪些数据

  • 你手动导入到 App 的本地视频文件（如 mp4 / m4v / mov）。
  • 视频标题、时长等基础元数据，以及播放模式、音量等偏好设置。
  • 手机与手表之间手动同步时所需的视频标识与视频数据（会转码为适合手表播放的格式）。

这些数据只用于在你自己的设备上播放视频，以及你主动发起的手表同步。

English

What We Collect and Store

  • Local video files you manually import into the app (for example, mp4 / m4v / mov).
  • Basic metadata such as video title and duration, plus preferences like playback mode and volume.
  • Video identifiers and data needed for the watch sync you start manually (transcoded into a watch-friendly format).

This data is only used to play videos on your own devices and for watch sync you start manually.

中文

我们不会收集什么

  • 使用本 App 无需注册账号或登录个人资料。
  • 我们不会主动收集你的姓名、手机号、通讯录、定位、照片、麦克风或相机数据。
  • 相册仅在导入视频时由你主动选择具体视频，App 不会扫描或上传整本相册。
  • 我们不会在应用中加入广告追踪，也不会把你的视频文件或使用数据出售给第三方。
English

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in to a profile.
  • We do not intentionally collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera data.
  • Photos are only accessed when you select a specific video to import; we never scan or upload your whole photo library.
  • We do not use ad tracking in the app or sell your video files or usage data to third parties.
中文

第三方服务与系统能力

  • Apple StoreKit：用于处理永久会员的一次性非消耗型 App 内购买，商品 ID 为 com.zsz.WatchVideo.PermanentMember。
  • Apple Watch Connectivity：用于在手机和手表之间同步视频列表与视频数据。
  • 系统文件选择器与相册选择器：用于让你手动选择并导入本地视频文件。

我们不会直接获取你的银行卡号、支付密码等敏感支付信息；购买流程由 Apple 负责。

English

Third-Party Services and System Features

  • Apple StoreKit: used for the one-time non-consumable lifetime membership in-app purchase with product ID com.zsz.WatchVideo.PermanentMember.
  • Apple Watch Connectivity: used to sync the video list and video data between iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • System file picker and Photos picker: used when you manually select local video files to import.

We do not directly access your card number, payment password, or other sensitive payment details. Apple handles the purchase flow.

中文

数据保留与删除

你的视频文件、播放记录和设置主要保存在本地设备上。手机与手表的视频库相互独立，你可以分别在两端删除视频。删除 App、移除视频或重置设备都会清除相关本地数据。

English

Data Retention and Deletion

Your video files, playback history, and settings are primarily stored on your local devices. The iPhone and Apple Watch libraries are independent, so you can delete videos on either device separately. Deleting the app, removing videos, or resetting the device will clear the related local data.
