中文

我们对隐私的态度

叶子日记是一款养花记录与提醒 App。本政策说明我们在你使用过程中如何处理相关数据。

English

Our Privacy Commitment

Leaf Diary is a plant care journal and reminder app. This policy explains how we handle the data you create while using it.

中文

我们会保存哪些数据

  • 你添加的植物资料：名称、品种、摆放位置、光照偏好、入手日期、备注和封面照片。
  • 养护记录：浇水、施肥、除虫、修剪等动作的类型、时间、备注，以及你附上的照片。
  • 提醒规则：类型、循环间隔或仅提醒一次、开始日期、提醒时间、上次完成时间，以及是否发送系统通知。
  • 你自定义的养护类型：名称、图标和颜色。
  • 高级会员的购买状态，用于判断植物数量限制是否已解锁。

以上数据用于在 App 内展示和管理你的植物与养护记录。

English

What We Store

  • Plant details you add: name, species, location, light preference, acquired date, notes, and cover photo.
  • Care entries: the type of action such as watering, fertilizing, pest control, or pruning, along with its date, note, and any photos you attach.
  • Reminder rules: type, repeating interval or one-time, start date, time of day, last completion date, and whether a system notification is sent.
  • Custom care types you create: name, icon, and color.
  • Premium purchase status, used to determine whether the plant limit is unlocked.

This data is used to display and manage your plants and care entries inside the app.

中文

照片与相机

  • 选择照片时使用系统提供的相册选择器，App 只能读取你主动挑选的那几张，无法浏览你的整个相册。
  • 你挑选的照片会复制一份到 App 内，用于记录和图库展示。
  • 「保存到相册」需要「仅添加」权限，只有你主动点击时才写入系统相册。

本 App 不会访问相机。

English

Photos and Camera

  • Choosing photos uses the system photo picker, so the app can only read the specific photos you select. It cannot browse your library.
  • The photos you pick are copied into the app for entries and the gallery.
  • Saving to your library uses add-only permission, and only happens when you tap Save to Photos yourself.

The app does not access the camera.

中文

通知

浇水施肥的提醒使用系统通知。到期时间根据你的养护记录计算，通知内容包含植物名称和动作。你可以随时在 App 内关闭单条提醒的通知，或在系统「设置 → 通知」里整体关闭。

English

Notifications

Watering and fertilizing reminders use system notifications. Due dates are calculated from your care entries, and the notification text includes the plant name and the action. You can turn off notifications for an individual reminder in the app, or disable them entirely in Settings, Notifications.

中文

我们不会收集什么

  • 我们不会收集你的姓名、手机号、通讯录、定位、麦克风或相机数据。
  • App 内没有广告，没有第三方分析或追踪 SDK。
  • 我们不会把你的植物记录或照片出售、分享给任何第三方。
English

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, microphone, or camera data.
  • There are no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • We never sell or share your plant records or photos with anyone.
中文

第三方服务与系统能力

  • Apple StoreKit：用于处理高级会员（永久）的一次性非消耗型 App 内购买，产品 ID 为 com.zsz.Plant.menber。
  • 系统相册选择器：用于让你挑选要附加到记录或设为封面的照片。
  • 系统通知：用于在到期时提醒你浇水、施肥等。

购买流程由 Apple 负责，我们不会接触你的银行卡号、支付密码等敏感支付信息，也拿不到你的 Apple ID。

English

Third-Party Services and System Features

  • Apple StoreKit: used for the one-time non-consumable Premium purchase with product ID com.zsz.Plant.menber.
  • System photo picker: used when you choose photos to attach to an entry or set as a cover.
  • System notifications: used to remind you when watering or fertilizing is due.

Apple handles the purchase flow. We never see your card number, payment credentials, or Apple ID.

中文

数据保留与删除

你可以在 App 内删除单条记录、整株植物或自定义类型，对应的照片也会一并删除。删除 App 会清除其中的植物资料、养护记录和照片，且无法恢复，请谨慎操作。

English

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete an individual entry, a whole plant, or a custom type inside the app, and the associated photos are removed as well. Deleting the app erases your plants, care entries, and photos permanently, so please be careful.

中文

导出与备份

你可以在 App 的「设置 → 备份」里把全部植物资料、养护记录和照片导出成一个 zip 文件，并自行决定存放到哪里、发送给谁。导入同一份备份时按 id 合并，已有的内容不会被覆盖或删除。

English

Export and Backup

In Settings, Backup, you can export all of your plants, care entries, and photos into a single zip file, and then decide where to store it or who to send it to. Importing a backup merges by ID, so existing content is never overwritten or deleted.
