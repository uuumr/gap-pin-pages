常见问题

支持哪些漫画格式？

支持 cbz 和 zip 两种压缩包（cbz 本质上就是改了扩展名的 zip）。cbr / cb7 / cbt / rar / 7z 暂不支持，请先转换成 zip 或 cbz 再导入。压缩包里需要有图片，否则会提示找不到图片。

怎么把漫画传到手表？

先在 iPhone 上导入漫画，打开「同步」标签页勾选要传的漫画，点「开始同步」即可；也可以在书架上长按某本漫画，直接选「传输到手表」。请保持手机与手表靠近，并先打开过一次手表 App。

传输一直停在「等待手表确认」？

请保持手机与手表连接，并让手表 App 停留在前台。若长时间没有进展，请重新打开两端 App 后重试。选择压缩档位时，传输前需要先处理每一页，页数多或选了高画质时首次会慢一些。

手表上字太小看不清怎么办？

在手表阅读器里轻点屏幕唤出控件，底部有放大和缩小按钮，可以按 0.1 倍逐步调整（1.0× 到 3.0×）；点中间的倍数标签可以一键回到 1.0×。放大后左右拖动即可查看页面其余部分。

怎样让同步到手表的内容更小？

在「设置」或「同步」页选择传输画质：原图（不压缩）、高清（长边约 1440 像素）、省空间（长边约 800 像素）。压缩只会让文件变小——如果压缩后反而更大，App 会自动改用原图发送。

手机上导入有数量限制吗？

手机上导入和阅读都不限数量。免费版只有同步到手表时有额度：最多 2 本，开通永久会员后手机和手表都不限数量。

阅读进度会同步吗？

手机和手表各自记录自己的阅读进度，互不覆盖：手机上读到第几页会显示在书架封面上，手表上读到第几页会显示在手表列表里。

可以只删除手表上的漫画吗？

可以。手表列表里每条漫画右侧有删除按钮，删除只影响手表，不影响手机上的书库；同样，手机上删除也不会自动删除手表上已同步的那本。

会员购买后没有生效？

可以点击「设置」中会员区块的「恢复购买」，并确认 Apple ID 登录状态与购买时一致。如果商品信息没有加载出来，那一行本身就是「重新加载商品」按钮，点一下即可重试。

FAQ

Which comic formats are supported?

cbz and zip archives (cbz is just a zip with a different extension). cbr / cb7 / cbt / rar / 7z are not supported yet — please convert them to zip or cbz first. The archive must contain images, otherwise the app reports that no pages were found.

How do I get comics onto my watch?

Import the comic on iPhone, open the Sync tab, select the comics you want, and tap Start Sync. You can also long-press a comic on the shelf and choose "Send to Apple Watch". Keep the devices close and open the watch app at least once first.

Why is the transfer stuck at "waiting for the watch"?

Keep the devices connected and the watch app in the foreground. If nothing advances, reopen both apps and try again. When a compressed quality is selected every page is processed before transfer, so the first sync can be slow for long comics or high quality settings.

The text is too small to read on the watch.

Tap the screen in the watch reader to reveal the controls. The zoom-out and zoom-in buttons at the bottom adjust the scale in steps of 0.1 (from 1.0x to 3.0x); tap the multiplier label to jump back to 1.0x. Once zoomed in, drag sideways to see the rest of the page.

How can I make synced comics smaller?

Pick a transfer quality in Settings or on the Sync screen: Original (no compression), High Quality (long edge about 1440 px), or Compact (long edge about 800 px). Compression only ever shrinks a file: if the result would be larger, the app sends the original instead.

Is there a limit on how many comics I can import?

No — importing and reading on iPhone is unlimited. The free tier only limits how many comics can be synced to Apple Watch: up to 2. Lifetime membership removes the limit on both devices.

Does reading progress sync between devices?

iPhone and Apple Watch keep their own reading progress and never overwrite each other. The page you reached shows on the shelf cover on iPhone and in the comic list on the watch.

Can I delete a comic from the watch only?

Yes. Each row in the watch list has a delete button, and deleting there does not affect your iPhone library. Likewise, deleting on iPhone does not remove the copy already synced to the watch.

Why didn't membership unlock after purchase?

Tap Restore Purchases in the membership section of Settings, and make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase. If the product info hasn't loaded, that row is itself a Reload Product button — tap it to retry.
