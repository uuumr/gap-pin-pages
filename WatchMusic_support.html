常见问题

无法导入音乐文件？

请确认文件格式为 mp3 / m4a / aac / wav / aiff / flac。如果仍然无法导入，可以尝试把文件压缩成 zip 后再次导入。

手表上没有同步的歌曲？

请在 iPhone 上点“开始同步”，并确认手表 App 已打开过一次、手机与手表处于可连接状态。同步需要保持两者靠近。

传输一直停在“等待手表确认”？

请保持手机与手表连接并让手表 App 停留在前台，传输过程中手表端会显示接收进度。若长时间无进展，请重新打开两端 App 后重试。

手表上歌曲不能播放或没有声音？

请确认已同步的歌曲在手表上能显示封面信息，并检查手表是否处于静音或勿扰状态。

非会员数量限制是多少？

非会员在手机上最多 3 首、手表上最多 2 首。开通永久会员后手机与手表均不限数量。

会员购买后没有生效？

可以点击设置中的“恢复购买”，并确认 Apple ID 登录状态与购买时一致。

FAQ

Why can't I import a music file?

Please make sure the file is mp3 / m4a / aac / wav / aiff / flac. If it still won't import, try zipping the file and importing the zip instead.

Why are there no songs on the watch?

Tap Start Sync on your iPhone, and make sure the watch app has been opened at least once while both devices are connected and nearby.

Why is the transfer stuck at "waiting for the watch"?

Keep the devices connected and the watch app in the foreground; the watch shows receiving progress during transfer. If nothing advances, reopen both apps and try again.

Why won't a song play or make sound on the watch?

Make sure the synced song appears in the watch library, and check that the watch is not muted or in Do Not Disturb.

What are the free-tier limits?

Free users can keep up to 3 songs on iPhone and 2 songs on Apple Watch. Lifetime membership removes the limits on both devices.

Why didn't membership unlock after purchase?

Try Restore Purchases in Settings, and make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.
