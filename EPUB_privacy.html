EPUB/TXT Converter converts and reads ebooks entirely on your device. Conversion, chapter detection, layout, cover generation and reading all happen locally. The books you import and your reading history are never uploaded, and we do not operate any server that stores them.

我们会在本机保存哪些数据

  • 你导入的书籍文件：TXT 与 EPUB 原文件，以及由本 App 转换生成的 EPUB 或 TXT。
  • 书籍信息：书名、作者、格式、文本编码、章节数、字数、文件大小、导入时间。
  • 封面图片：自动生成的封面，以及你从相册中选用作为封面的图片。
  • 阅读数据：阅读进度（章节与页码位置），以及你的阅读设置（字号、行距、页边距、主题、字体）。
  • 你导出的文件：由本 App 生成、保存在 Exports 目录中等待你分享或储存的 EPUB / TXT。
  • 会员状态：用于判断永久会员是否已解锁的购买标记。

以上内容全部存放在 App 自己的沙盒目录中，仅用于在 App 内展示、转换与阅读。

English

What We Store on Your Device

  • Books you import: the original TXT and EPUB files, plus any EPUB or TXT this app produces.
  • Book details: title, author, format, text encoding, chapter count, character count, file size, and import date.
  • Cover images: covers generated by the app, and any photo you pick from your library to use as a cover.
  • Reading data: your position in each book, and your reading settings such as font size, line spacing, margins, theme, and typeface.
  • Exported files: EPUB or TXT files created by the app and kept in the Exports folder until you share or save them.
  • Membership status: a purchase flag used to tell whether the lifetime membership is unlocked.

All of this lives inside the app sandbox and is used only to display, convert, and read your books inside the app.

System Access and Features

  • File picker: the system file picker is used to import TXT or EPUB files and to save converted files to the Files app. The app can only see the files you explicitly choose.

You can delete a single book from the library, which removes its file and cover as well, and you can clear every exported file in Settings, Storage. Deleting the app erases your library, reading progress, reading settings, and exported files permanently, so please be careful.

中文

关于免费试用记录

为了判断 24 小时免费试用是否已经结束，App 会记录一个「首次使用时间」。这条记录保存在系统钥匙串（Keychain）中，因此删除并重装 App 后仍然存在，试用不会因此重新开始；它只包含时间信息，不包含任何个人身份内容。开通永久会员后，这条记录不再影响使用。

English

About the Free Trial Record

To tell whether the 24-hour free trial has ended, the app records the time of first use. This record is kept in the system Keychain, so it survives deleting and reinstalling the app and the trial does not restart. It contains only timestamps and no personal information. Once the lifetime membership is purchased, this record no longer affects anything.
