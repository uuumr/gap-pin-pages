中文

我们对隐私的态度

腕上漫画是一款本地离线的漫画阅读 App：你在 iPhone 上导入自己的漫画压缩包，阅读时可以按需翻页，也可以把漫画同步到 Apple Watch 上离线阅读。漫画文件默认只保存在你的设备本地，不会上传到任何服务器。我们只在你的设备之间处理必要的数据，不做账号体系，也不做广告追踪。

English

Our Privacy Commitment

Wrist Comics is a local, offline comic reader. You import your own comic archives on iPhone, read them page by page, and can sync them to your Apple Watch for offline reading. Your comics stay on your devices by default and are never uploaded to any server. We only process the data needed to deliver reading and syncing between your own devices, with no account system and no ad tracking.

中文

我们会收集和保存哪些数据

  • 你手动导入到 App 的漫画压缩包（cbz / zip）。
  • 漫画的标题、页数、封面缩略图，以及每本读到第几页、最近打开时间等阅读进度。
  • 你的偏好设置，例如同步到手表时使用的传输画质（原图 / 高清 / 省空间）和会员状态。
  • 手机与手表之间手动同步时所需的漫画标识与压缩包数据（选择压缩档位时，会在传输前生成适合手表的副本）。
  • 为节省传输体积而生成的手表版压缩包缓存，仅保存在本机缓存目录中。

这些数据只用于在你自己的设备上阅读漫画，以及你主动发起的手表同步。

English

What We Collect and Store

  • Comic archives (cbz / zip) you manually import into the app.
  • Comic titles, page counts and cover thumbnails, plus reading progress such as the last page you read and when you last opened a comic.
  • Your preferences, such as the transfer quality used when syncing to Apple Watch (Original / High Quality / Compact) and your membership status.
  • Comic identifiers and archive data needed for the watch sync you start manually; when you pick a compressed quality, a watch-friendly copy is generated before transfer.
  • Cached watch-format archives created to keep transfers small, stored only in the app's local cache directory.

This data is only used to read comics on your own devices and for the watch sync you start manually.

中文

我们不会收集什么

  • 使用本 App 无需注册账号或登录个人资料。
  • 我们不会主动收集你的姓名、手机号、通讯录、定位、照片、麦克风或相机数据。
  • 导入漫画只通过系统文件选择器读取你主动选中的压缩包；App 不会扫描你的相册、照片或整个文件系统。
  • 我们不会在应用中加入广告追踪，也不会把你的漫画文件或使用数据出售给第三方。
English

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in to a profile.
  • We do not intentionally collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera data.
  • Comics are imported only through the system file picker and only for the archive you explicitly choose; the app never scans your photo library or your file system.
  • We do not use ad tracking in the app or sell your comic files or usage data to third parties.

English

Third-Party Services and System Features

  • Apple StoreKit: used for the one-time non-consumable lifetime membership in-app purchase with product ID com.zsz.Manhua.member.1.
  • Apple Watch Connectivity: used to sync the comic list and archive data between iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • System file picker: used when you manually select local cbz / zip comic archives to import.

We do not directly access your card number, payment password, or other sensitive payment details. Apple handles the purchase flow.

中文

数据保留与删除

你的漫画文件、阅读进度和设置主要保存在本地设备上。手机与手表的漫画库相互独立，你可以分别在两端删除漫画。删除 App、移除漫画文件或重置设备都会清除相关本地数据；系统在空间紧张时也可能清理手表版压缩包缓存。

English

Data Retention and Deletion

Your comic files, reading progress, and settings are primarily stored on your local devices. The iPhone and Apple Watch libraries are independent, so you can delete comics on either device separately. Deleting the app, removing comics, or resetting the device will clear the related local data; the system may also purge cached watch-format archives when storage runs low.
