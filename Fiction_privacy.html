中文

我们对隐私的态度

腕上小说阅读器是一款本地小说阅读 App，主打 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 之间的轻量阅读体验。我们尽量只在你的设备之间处理必要数据，不会为了经营广告或账号体系而额外收集个人信息。

English

Our Privacy Commitment

Wrist Novel Reader is a local novel reading app designed for iPhone and Apple Watch. We aim to process only the data needed to deliver the reading experience, and we do not build advertising or account systems that require unnecessary personal data.

中文

我们会收集和保存哪些数据

  • 你手动导入到 App 的本地 TXT 或其他文本小说文件。
  • 书名、作者、章节信息、阅读进度、字号、行距、页边距、主题等阅读设置。
  • 手机和手表之间手动同步时所需的书籍标识、正文内容、章节分页信息与进度数据。
  • 高级会员购买状态，用于判断是否解锁手机 3 本、手表 1 本的限制。

以上数据主要保存在你的设备本地，并通过 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 之间的系统通信进行同步。

English

What Data We Collect and Store

  • Local TXT or other text novels that you manually import into the app.
  • Book titles, authors, chapter information, reading progress, font size, line spacing, page margin, and theme preferences.
  • Book identifiers, full text, chapter pagination data, and progress data needed for manual syncing between iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Premium membership status, which is used to determine whether the 3-book phone limit and 1-book watch limit are unlocked.

Most of this data is stored locally on your devices and is synchronized through Apple's system communication between iPhone and Apple Watch.

中文

我们不会主动收集什么

  • 我们不会要求你注册账号或登录个人资料。
  • 我们不会主动收集你的姓名、手机号、通讯录、定位、照片、麦克风或相机数据。
  • 我们不会在应用中加入广告追踪或把你的阅读内容出售给第三方。
English

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in to a profile.
  • We do not intentionally collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera data.
  • We do not use ad tracking in the app or sell your reading content to third parties.
中文

第三方服务与系统能力

  • Apple StoreKit：用于处理高级会员的一次性非消耗型 App 内购买，产品 ID 为 com.zsz.Fiction.member.6。
  • Apple Watch Connectivity：用于在手机和手表之间同步书架、正文和阅读进度。
  • 系统文件选择器：用于让你手动选择并导入本地小说文件。

我们不会直接获取你的银行卡号、支付密码等敏感支付信息；购买流程由 Apple 负责。

English

Third-Party Services and System Features

  • Apple StoreKit: used for the one-time non-consumable premium in-app purchase with product ID com.zsz.Fiction.member.6.
  • Apple Watch Connectivity: used to sync the bookshelf, full text, and reading progress between iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • System file picker: used when you manually select local novel files to import.

We do not directly access your card number, payment password, or other sensitive payment details. Apple handles the purchase flow.

中文

数据保留与删除

你的书籍内容、阅读记录和设置主要保存在本地设备上。你可以通过删除 App、移除书籍或重置设备来清除相关数据。若你删除应用，本地数据可能会一并移除。

English

Data Retention and Deletion

Your book content, reading history, and settings are primarily stored on your local devices. You can remove the related data by deleting the app, removing books, or resetting the device. If you delete the app, local data may be removed as well.
